================================================================================
Exercise 05: Replace Reduce with Allreduce
================================================================================

Description:
MPI_Reduce delivers the final answer to root only. If worker ranks also need
the total sum (e.g. to calculate their percentage contribution to the whole),
a subsequent MPI_Bcast would normally be required.

In Exercise 5, MPI_Allreduce combines both reduction and broadcast into a
single, highly optimized collective call:
1. MPI_Allreduce:
   - MPI_Allreduce(&local_sum, &total_sum, 1, MPI_LONG_LONG, MPI_SUM, MPI_COMM_WORLD);
   - Notice: No root argument is provided; all processes receive the result.
   - Typically implemented using butterfly / recursive-doubling algorithms.
2. Contribution Percentage:
   - Since every process possesses total_sum, each process computes:
     percentage = ((double)local_sum / (double)total_sum) * 100.0;
   - Each process prints its rank, local_sum, total_sum, and percentage.
